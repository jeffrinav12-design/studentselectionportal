REPORT SUMMARY

The Department of Computer Science with Data Analytics organized the official digital selection process for Semester VI Project Guide Allocation and Elective Courses exclusively for the students of III B.Sc CSDA (Batch 2024–2027). The selection was conducted via custom-designed software engineered by Data Sorcerers. The event was aimed at equipping the department with an automated, impartial, and transparent system for allocating 41 project students across four faculty guide members and determining the most preferred elective courses for 43 students.

INTRODUCTION

An automated, cryptographically randomized allocation initiative was conducted by the Department of Computer Science with Data Analytics as a part of its academic curriculum enrichment programs. The session focused on executing project guide allotment with high fairness and zero preferential bias through a Dynamic Anti-Collision Slot Shuffling Engine, ensuring that students selecting identical slots receive rotated guide mappings.

PARTICIPANTS

The target audience was the III B.Sc CSDA students (enrolled strength of 41 students for Project Guide Selection and 43 students for Elective Selection). The process saw active, enthusiastic, and 100% transparent participation across multiple devices connected via local network Wi-Fi.

PROGRAM FLOW & METHODOLOGY

The session began with an orientation on the software architecture and dynamic shuffling rules. Students registered using their official college Register Number and Full Name. Upon clicking a mystery slot, the underlying engine dynamically mapped an eligible faculty guide whose quota was not yet saturated. Real-time anti-collision rules guaranteed that if a previous student received a guide from a specific slot, the very next student clicking that same slot received a dynamically varied guide.
